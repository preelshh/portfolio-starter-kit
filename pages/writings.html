what i think constitutes good writing

i think for me, the mark of a really good piece of media is always in its writing. there are four points i personally search for

1) well written characters

this is such a broad category for me. i know i have a bit of a preference for characters who are often aware of their own flaws and this self awarness actually propels their own deterioation, but there's so much i really love.

well-written characters also leads to a compounding effect of well-written relationships within media. specifically, i like relationships that touch on the theme of imbalance. i wonder in this world if there exists a relationship that is ever balanced totally. its surely impossible. we all pretend and go about in common society that relationshpis are balanced, but the truth is, there are complex dynamics, hierarchys, and resultant subversions to every connection in our life. i like it hen media tackles it head on.

finally, for what i define a 10/10 isn't actually if it hits a 10/10 on every category here. i think when you engage at the right time and place and theres a connection to the material, it will stick with you forever. my 10/10s arent the best written but perhaps the ones that i cannot forget.

1:40 am; august 8th, 2026 [gravity falls]

overall rating: 10/10

just finished gravity falls rewatch 4. i was inspired to watch the show as ive been chilling here in the pacific northwest for the SUMMER. essentially, there was no way i could not watch the show.

i think for me personally this show has probably shaped my taste in media since i was ten years old. in particular, i have a disdain for episodic shows. the beauty of gravity falls and why it is a show that has stood the test of time for DECADES is because of its serialization. i also use this show to corroborate my growing evidence that animated shows invoke the writers to do a much better job of writing serialized tv, planning out dramatic plot events from episode one, and conceptualizing the character trajectory from the beginning as well. im not sure why as i am not an animator but i think its because animation is a more detail-oriented form of media (generally), just because of its raw production.

anyways, i believe more shows should be written in an episodic format. more games should be developed in an episodic format. sometimes, i feel like even movies need to be written in an episodic format...

on a different note, i think rewatching shows and retroactively analyzing your personal thoughts from the show is a great way to track your own development as a person.

like, for example, i watched this show when i was 10, 14 (probably), 17?, and now 21.

from 10-17 i definetly thought i was mabel pines. now, i couldnt possibly think i am like that truly on the inside. i still love people i guess but im surely more skeptical now and value working on myself a bit more too. i relate to him nature of his hobbies and his general cynical nature. suprsingly, most people in my life would think this is a crazy diaonsis of myself! including my 10 year old self but, you know.

to cope, i am watching gravity falls video essays, and this fellow said this:

"gravity falls is one of the best animated shows you've ever seen. it is infintiesly rewatchable, funny, sinespneful. it is the aniamted medium proving once again that animation could not only support a story, but dothings that other mediums simply can't."

i felt happy to see this because its a idea i implore to anyone who will listen. that the writing talent and world building in animation is remarkable and deserves infinite chances.
