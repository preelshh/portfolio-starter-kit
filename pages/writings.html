writings on fiction

what i think constitutes good writing

i think for me, the mark of a really good piece of media is always in its writing. there are four points i personally search for

1) well written characters

this is such a broad category for me. i know i have a bit of a preference for characters who are often aware of their own flaws and this self awarness actually propels their own deterioation, but there's so much i really love.

well-written characters also leads to a compounding effect of well-written relationships within media. specifically, i like relationships that touch on the theme of imbalance. i wonder in this world if there exists a relationship that is ever balanced totally. its surely impossible. we all pretend and go about in common society that relationshpis are balanced, but the truth is, there are complex dynamics, hierarchys, and resultant subversions to every connection in our life. i like it hen media tackles it head on.

2) an interesting plot, one with good action, twists and development

good examples: gravity falls, hunter x hunter, mob psycho, arcane, hunger games, bastard, season 3 of stranger things, deathnote, last of us, olgami, look back, shutter island, many more but i invested most of my time in well written characters

bad examples: barbie from barbie, devil wears prada 2

3) good world building

good examples: hunter x hunter, one piece, arcane, percy jackson, the wire, harry potter, gravity falls, friends, hunger games, hand jumper, sucession, last of us, detroit become human, undertale/deltarune, legend of zelda breath of the wild

4) an interesting complex and nuanced overall message shown through a variety of correlating themes

good examples: i think everything i put in the characters section honestly. good character writing usually comes with good thematic writing.

bad examples: thirteen reasons why

finally, for what i define a 10/10 isn't actually if it hits a 10/10 on every category here. i think when you engage at the right time and place and theres a connection to the material, it will stick with you forever. my 10/10s arent the best written but perhaps the ones that i cannot forget.
